about

Hi, I'm Ricky.

An Indonesian born and raised designer / artist working with visuals mainly in computer graphics.

My practice revolves around visual exploration, but more importantly, around understanding the structures behind what we see. I believe meaningful work emerges from deep immersion — like a craftsman devoted to their crafts, combined with the cumulative, non-trivial growth of knowledge that defines science. Research in mathematics, in that sense, is my muse and collaborator. Shaped early on by 3 books : Lumen Naturae by Matilde Marcolli, The Primacy of Doubt by Tim Palmer, Math Art by Stephen Ornes and the online course The Shape of Nature by Satyan L. Devadoss, I aim to pursue research in mathematics with a particular interest in geometry processing, dynamical systems and geometric folding algorithms.

This website is setup as an ongoing collection of experiments, studies, and questions — a record of my journey told through selected projects.
