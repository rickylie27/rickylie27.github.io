Length of Project ~ 3 months

A cinematic 3D product animation showcasing a functional bio-filtration system, driven almost entirely by procedural generation and physics-based simulations.

Technical

Parametric Modelling

Geometries produced by a parametric periodic function that utilises sinusoidal terms followed by a combination of affine transformations, duplication and subdivision.

This technique is used to deconstruct and recreate an actual object into a virtual geometry that is used in both simulations and renderings.

FLIP - Rigid Body Dynamics - Particle Simulations

Multiple-tier rigid body simulation with movements dictated by advection to mimic movements in a body of fluid. Furthermore, a variation of the constructed advection is used for position-based geometries deformation (bubbles).

High resolution, CPU-based Fluid Implicit Particle (FLIP) method of fluid simulations that interacts with rigid body geometries.

Fabric Simulation - Geometry and UV Project

The constructed geometry is projected onto the XZ plane and that plane is used as a UV coordinate.

An animated geometry (sphere) is used as a collider to a vellum cloth simulation and we project the previously obtained plane to attain the final result.
